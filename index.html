About This Age Calculator

This calculator computes your exact age accounting for leap years, varying month lengths (28/29/30/31 days) and the precise number of days. Unlike simple year subtraction, it gives you years + months + remaining days.

It also shows your total days lived (great for "I'm 10,000 days old today" celebrations), next birthday countdown, zodiac sign, and birth stone.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is exact age calculated?

The calculator finds years by checking if this year's birthday has passed. Then counts remaining months, then remaining days — always using actual calendar month lengths.

What if I was born on Feb 29 (leap day)?

In non-leap years, your birthday is counted on Feb 28. The calculator handles this automatically.

Can I calculate age on a past or future date?

Yes — use the "Age on Date" field. Enter any date and the calculator shows how old you were or will be on that date.
