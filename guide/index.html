  • Driving licence (LMV): 18 years
  • Voting: 18 years
  • Marriage (female): 18 years; (male): 21 years
  • Senior citizen (bank FD rates): 60 years
  • Central govt retirement: 60 years
  • NPS exit: 60 years (can defer to 75)
  • Senior citizen income tax benefit: 60 years (super senior: 80 years)

Frequently Asked Questions

How is exact age calculated?

Years = current year − birth year (subtract 1 if birthday hasn't occurred this year). Remaining months and days are calculated by comparing month/day of birth to today, accounting for leap years and varying month lengths.

What is the retirement age in India?

Central government employees retire at 60. State governments vary (58–62). NPS allows exit at 60 with option to defer to 75. Use the age calculator to see exactly how many years, months and days remain to your retirement.

How do I calculate age difference between two people?

Use the Difference tab, enter both dates of birth, and the calculator shows the exact difference in years, months and days.
